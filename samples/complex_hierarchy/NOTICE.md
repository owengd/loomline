These three files are the `complex_hierarchy` demo project from the KiCad source tree
(https://gitlab.com/kicad/code/kicad, `demos/complex_hierarchy`), included unmodified as a
sample. They are distributed under the same terms as KiCad (GNU GPL v3 or later).
Delete this folder if you don't want the sample in your deployment; the "Try the sample"
card hides itself when the files are missing.
